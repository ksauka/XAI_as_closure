# AI Governance Lead — Job Description
*(Recruiter-facing knowledge document. The recruiter may inspect it at any time.)*

## Section 1. Role Designation
**1.1 Title.** AI Governance Lead.
**1.2 Nature of Role.** An in-house governance and compliance role responsible for the formal risk and conformity assessment of the bank's high-risk AI systems, including the AI systems supporting loan assessment and investment recommendations, supported by advisory and stakeholder work.

## Section 2. Purpose
**2.1 Primary Purpose.** Own the governance of the bank's AI systems, including formal AI risk and conformity assessment, control frameworks, and regulatory compliance under the EU AI Act.
**2.2 Positioning.** The role sits between the risk function, data science, compliance, and legal.
**2.3 Regulatory Function.** The role ensures the bank's high-risk AI systems are assessed, documented, and compliant, reducing regulatory and reputational exposure.

## Section 3. Core Responsibilities
**3.1 Risk and Conformity Assessment.** Conduct and sign off formal AI risk and conformity assessments for high-risk AI systems under a recognised framework.
**3.2 Governance Frameworks.** Design and maintain governance frameworks and control mechanisms for the bank's AI systems.
**3.3 Compliance.** Translate regulatory requirements, including the EU AI Act, into practical controls and documentation.
**3.4 Advisory and Stakeholder Work.** Advise data science, risk, compliance, and legal on Responsible AI, and manage stakeholders across those functions.
**3.5 Execution.** Operate with limited supervision while maintaining documentation and audit readiness.

## Section 4. Mandatory Requirement
**4.1 Mandatory Certification.** The candidate must hold a current, recognised professional AI governance certification, being either the IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) or the ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer certification.
**4.2 Disqualifying Effect.** A candidate who does not hold either certification must not be advanced, regardless of other strengths.
**4.3 Non-Substitutability.** Experience, advisory work, or training in AI governance does not substitute for the certification in Section 4.1.

## Section 5. General Requirements
**5.1 Assessment Experience.** At least three years of experience conducting or supporting formal AI risk or conformity assessment under a recognised framework such as the EU AI Act or ISO/IEC 42001.
**5.2 Advisory Experience.** At least two years of experience advising on AI governance, Responsible AI, or AI risk and compliance.
**5.3 Technical Understanding.** Working understanding of AI and machine-learning systems, including generative AI and enterprise architecture.
**5.4 Consulting and Stakeholder Management.** At least two years of consulting and stakeholder-management experience across business, compliance, and technical functions.

## Section 6. Team and Reporting Structure
**6.1 Reporting Line.** The AI Governance Lead reports to the Head of Model Risk and Governance within the second-line Risk function, with a dotted line into the Chief Compliance Office for regulatory-facing deliverables.
**6.2 Team Context.** The role sits within a small, specialist AI governance team of three to five people, working alongside model validators, data protection officers, and the enterprise architecture group. The team is expected to grow as the bank's AI Act obligations mature.
**6.3 Cross-Functional Touchpoints.** The role works routinely with data science teams building or maintaining high-risk AI systems, with legal counsel on regulatory interpretation, and with internal audit during assurance cycles.
**6.4 Decision Rights.** The role has authority to require remediation of a governance gap before a high-risk AI system proceeds to deployment, but escalates disputed sign-offs to the Head of Model Risk and Governance rather than deciding unilaterally.

## Section 7. Day-to-Day Activities
**7.1 Assessment Cadence.** Formal risk and conformity assessments are typically conducted on a rolling basis across the bank's inventory of high-risk AI systems, with priority given to systems nearing a release or material change.
**7.2 Documentation and Audit Trail.** The role maintains assessment records, control mappings, and sign-off documentation to a standard suitable for internal audit and external regulatory inspection.
**7.3 Committee and Governance Forums.** The role prepares materials for and attends the bank's AI governance committee, presenting assessment outcomes, open risks, and recommended controls to senior stakeholders.
**7.4 Incident and Escalation Support.** Where a deployed AI system is suspected of producing a materially incorrect or unfair outcome, the role supports the incident review, including identifying whether an existing conformity assessment remains valid.
**7.5 Framework Maintenance.** The role periodically reviews and updates the bank's internal AI governance framework and control library to reflect regulatory guidance and internal lessons learned.

## Section 8. Working Arrangements
**8.1 Location and Hybrid Working.** The role is based at the bank's head office, with a hybrid working pattern of at least three days per week on-site to support committee attendance and cross-functional work.
**8.2 Travel.** Occasional travel to other bank sites may be required to support assessments of AI systems developed or operated outside the head office.
**8.3 Time-Sensitive Reviews.** The role is occasionally required to turn around a conformity assessment on a compressed timeline ahead of a regulatory deadline or a scheduled system release, and to prioritise accordingly.

## Section 9. Desired Additional Experience
**9.1 Sector Exposure.** Prior experience in financial services, or another sector subject to comparable high-risk AI regulation, is desirable but not required.
**9.2 Toolset Familiarity.** Familiarity with model documentation, risk-register, or governance-workflow tooling is an advantage.
**9.3 Communication Skills.** Ability to explain technical AI risk concepts to non-technical senior stakeholders in written and verbal form.
**9.4 Prior Regulatory Engagement.** Experience engaging directly with a regulator or external auditor on an AI-related or model-risk matter is a plus.

## Section 10. Performance Expectations
**10.1 First-Year Objectives.** In the first year, the role is expected to complete conformity assessments for the bank's existing inventory of high-risk AI systems and to close any material control gaps identified.
**10.2 Ongoing Development.** The postholder is expected to maintain their professional certification and to stay current with evolving EU AI Act guidance and standards revisions relevant to the role.
**10.3 Career Progression.** Strong performance in this role is a recognised path toward a senior AI governance or Head of Model Risk position within the bank.

## Section 11. Equal Opportunity and Application
**11.1 Equal Opportunity Statement.** Suvh Trust Bank is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applications regardless of background, consistent with applicable law.
**11.2 Application Process.** Applications are reviewed against this job description and the accompanying recruitment policy; shortlisted candidates proceed to a first-stage screening review followed by a structured interview.
**11.3 Pre-Employment Checks.** An offer of employment is subject to standard financial-services pre-employment checks, including verification of the certification referenced in Section 4.1.

## Section 12. About Suvh Trust Bank
**12.1 Overview.** Suvh Trust Bank is a mid-sized retail and commercial bank offering personal banking, business lending, and investment advisory services, with a multi-year programme underway to expand its use of AI across underwriting and advisory functions.
**12.2 AI Programme Context.** The bank's AI systems support loan assessment, investment recommendations, and internal risk analytics. Several of these systems are classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act, which is the direct driver for this role.
**12.3 Governance Maturity.** The bank's AI governance function was established two years ago and is scaling its assessment capacity to meet upcoming regulatory deadlines; this role is a core part of that scale-up.

## Section 13. Compensation and Benefits Overview
**13.1 Compensation Structure.** Compensation for this role comprises a base salary benchmarked to comparable second-line risk and governance roles in financial services, plus an annual performance-related bonus.
**13.2 Benefits.** Standard benefits include pension contribution, private healthcare, and a professional development allowance that may be used toward maintaining the certification referenced in Section 4.1.
**13.3 Study and Certification Support.** Where a postholder's certification requires periodic renewal or continuing-education credits, the bank supports the associated cost and reasonable study time.

## Section 14. Key Interfaces
**14.1 Model Validation.** The role works with the model validation team to ensure that conformity assessments are consistent with independent model-risk findings for the same AI system.
**14.2 Data Protection Office.** The role coordinates with the Data Protection Office where a high-risk AI system's conformity assessment overlaps with a data protection impact assessment.
**14.3 External Auditors.** The role is a primary point of contact for external auditors and regulators reviewing the bank's high-risk AI systems, and prepares evidence packs for those reviews.
