# Suvh Trust Bank — AI Governance and Responsible AI Recruitment Policy
*(Governing knowledge document for screening candidates for AI governance and Responsible AI roles. The recruiter may inspect it at any time.)*

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope
**1.1 Purpose.** This Policy governs first-stage screening of candidates for the bank's AI governance and Responsible AI roles, including the AI Governance Lead.
**1.2 Context.** The bank deploys AI systems in high-risk decisions, including loan assessment and investment recommendations, and operates under the EU AI Act, which classifies AI used in creditworthiness assessment as high-risk. Screening for these roles must therefore be rigorous and evidence-based.
**1.3 Advisory Use of AI.** An AI-assisted system may review candidate materials and produce a screening recommendation, but acts only in an advisory capacity. The human recruiter makes the final decision and is accountable for it.

## Section 2. Screening Principles
**2.1 Requirements-First.** Screening shall determine whether the candidate satisfies all mandatory professional requirements and the general requirements for the role, each assessed against the candidate file. A candidate who does not satisfy every mandatory professional requirement shall be rejected regardless of other strengths. A candidate who does not meet the general requirements shall be rejected. A candidate who satisfies all mandatory professional requirements and the general requirements shall not be rejected on the basis of other strengths, comparative shortfalls, or attributes that are neither a mandatory professional requirement nor a general requirement.
**2.2 Evidence Rule.** Recommendations must be grounded only in information visible in this Policy, the job description, and the candidate file. No qualification or certification may be assumed without record support.
**2.3 Whole-File and Currency Rule.** The mandatory professional requirements shall be assessed against the whole candidate file and as of the date on which the candidate is screened. A certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description satisfies the certification requirement only where the whole file records it as current on that date, and the professional membership required by Section 4.2 must likewise be current. An expired, suspended, or revoked certification, or an absent or lapsed membership, does not satisfy the requirements; current status or renewal must not be assumed.
**2.4 Non-Substitution.** Adjacent, transferable, or training experience does not substitute for a mandatory professional certification or professional membership where the job description requires them. Certification and membership do not substitute for one another.

## Section 3. Permitted Outcomes
**3.1 Outcome Set.** The permitted first-stage outcomes are **Advance candidate to human interview** and **Reject candidate**. The decision is terminal; there is no deferral or hold outcome.
**3.2 Advance candidate to human interview.** Advance only where the candidate holds a current certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description, holds the current professional membership required by Section 4.2, and adequately meets the general requirements.
**3.3 Reject candidate.** Reject where the candidate does not hold a current certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description or the current professional membership required by Section 4.2, including where the certification is absent, expired, suspended, or revoked or the membership is absent or lapsed, regardless of other strengths.

## Section 4. Fairness and Evidential Limits
**4.1 No Assumed Evidence.** An AI-assisted recommendation shall not invent, infer, or assume an unsupported certification, professional membership, or qualification.
**4.2 No Protected-Attribute Reasoning.** No recommendation shall rest on actual or inferred protected personal characteristics. Candidates are identified by reference number.
**4.3 Accurate Representation.** Where a qualifying certification or required professional membership is absent or is not current, the recommendation shall not present a previously held certification, adjacent qualification, membership, or related experience as if it satisfied the mandatory professional requirements.

## Section 5. Human Decision Authority
**5.1 Final Decision.** The final screening decision remains with the human recruiter.
**5.2 Verification Right.** The recruiter may inspect this Policy, the job description, and the whole candidate file at any time before deciding, including sections the AI did not cite.
**5.3 Duty of Review.** The recruiter shall confirm the identity and current status of the mandatory certification and professional membership against the whole candidate file rather than treat the AI recommendation as self-sufficient.
