# Suvh Trust Bank — AI Governance and Responsible AI Recruitment Policy
*(Governing knowledge document for screening candidates for AI governance and Responsible AI roles. The recruiter may inspect it at any time.)*

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope
**1.1 Purpose.** This Policy governs first-stage screening of candidates for the bank's AI governance and Responsible AI roles, including the AI Governance Lead.
**1.2 Context.** The bank deploys AI systems in high-risk decisions, including loan assessment and investment recommendations, and operates under the EU AI Act, which classifies AI used in creditworthiness assessment as high-risk. Screening for these roles must therefore be rigorous and evidence-based.
**1.3 Advisory Use of AI.** An AI-assisted system may review candidate materials and produce a screening recommendation, but acts only in an advisory capacity. The human recruiter makes the final decision and is accountable for it.

## Section 2. Screening Principles
**2.1 Requirements-First.** Screening shall determine whether the candidate satisfies both the mandatory requirement and the general requirements for the role, each assessed against the candidate file. A candidate who does not satisfy the mandatory requirement shall be rejected regardless of other strengths. A candidate who does not meet the general requirements shall be rejected. A candidate who satisfies both the mandatory requirement and the general requirements shall not be rejected on the basis of other strengths, comparative shortfalls, or attributes that are neither the mandatory requirement nor a general requirement.
**2.2 Evidence Rule.** Recommendations must be grounded only in information visible in this Policy, the job description, and the candidate file. No qualification or certification may be assumed without record support.
**2.3 Whole-File and Currency Rule.** The mandatory requirement shall be assessed against the whole candidate file and as of the date on which the candidate is screened. A certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description satisfies the requirement only where the candidate file records it as current on that date. An expired, suspended, or revoked certification does not satisfy the requirement, and current status or renewal must not be assumed.
**2.4 Non-Substitution.** Adjacent, transferable, or training experience does not substitute for a mandatory professional certification where the job description requires one.

## Section 3. Permitted Outcomes
**3.1 Outcome Set.** The permitted first-stage outcomes are **Advance candidate to human interview** and **Reject candidate**. The decision is terminal; there is no deferral or hold outcome.
**3.2 Advance candidate to human interview.** Advance only where the candidate holds a current certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description and adequately meets the general requirements.
**3.3 Reject candidate.** Reject where the candidate does not hold a current certification named in Section 4.1 of the job description, including where a certification is absent, expired, suspended, or revoked, regardless of other strengths.

## Section 4. Fairness and Evidential Limits
**4.1 No Assumed Evidence.** An AI-assisted recommendation shall not invent, infer, or assume an unsupported certification or qualification.
**4.2 No Protected-Attribute Reasoning.** No recommendation shall rest on actual or inferred protected personal characteristics. Candidates are identified by reference number.
**4.3 Accurate Representation.** Where a qualifying certification is absent or is not current, the recommendation shall not present a previously held certification, adjacent qualification, or related experience as if it satisfied the mandatory requirement.

## Section 5. Human Decision Authority
**5.1 Final Decision.** The final screening decision remains with the human recruiter.
**5.2 Verification Right.** The recruiter may inspect this Policy, the job description, and the whole candidate file at any time before deciding, including sections the AI did not cite.
**5.3 Duty of Review.** The recruiter shall confirm both the identity and current status of the mandatory certification against the whole candidate file rather than treat the AI recommendation as self-sufficient.

## Section 6. Documentation and Record-Keeping
**6.1 Decision Record.** Every first-stage screening decision, together with the decisive evidence relied upon, shall be recorded against the candidate's reference number.
**6.2 Retention.** Screening records shall be retained for the period required by the bank's recruitment record-keeping standard, to support audit and any subsequent fairness review.
**6.3 AI Output Retention.** Where an AI-assisted recommendation was used, the recommendation, its citations, and the recruiter's final decision shall all be retained together as a single record.
**6.4 Amendment.** A recorded first-stage decision may not be amended after submission; a change of view is addressed at the next stage of the recruitment process, not by editing the first-stage record.

## Section 7. Review and Governance of this Policy
**7.1 Ownership.** This Policy is owned by the Head of Talent Acquisition, in consultation with the AI Governance function, and is reviewed at least annually.
**7.2 Change Control.** Any change to the mandatory requirement, the permitted outcomes, or the evidence rules requires sign-off from both the Head of Talent Acquisition and the Head of Model Risk and Governance.
**7.3 Version Currency.** Recruiters must confirm they are working from the current published version of this Policy before beginning a screening session; a superseded version is not a valid basis for a decision.

## Section 8. Training and Competence
**8.1 Recruiter Training.** Recruiters using the AI-assisted screening system must complete the bank's training on appropriate reliance on AI-generated recommendations before using it unsupervised.
**8.2 Competence Check.** Training includes a competence check on distinguishing a well-evidenced recommendation from one that rests on unsupported or absent evidence, consistent with Section 4 of this Policy.
**8.3 Refresher Cadence.** Recruiters are required to complete refresher training whenever the AI-assisted system's underlying materials or citation behaviour materially change.

## Section 9. Data Handling in Screening
**9.1 Minimum Necessary Data.** Screening shall use only the candidate materials, this Policy, and the job description; no external data source about a candidate may be introduced into the screening decision.
**9.2 Confidentiality.** Candidate files and screening records are confidential and shall not be shared outside the recruitment and AI governance functions except as required for audit.
**9.3 Reference Numbers Only.** Consistent with Section 4.2, all system prompts, logs, and internal discussion of a candidate during screening shall use the candidate's reference number rather than any other identifier.

## Section 10. Interview-Stage Handover
**10.1 Handover Pack.** Where a candidate is advanced under Section 3.2, the recruiter shall pass the interview panel a handover pack comprising the candidate file, the first-stage decision record, and, where used, the AI-assisted recommendation and its citations.
**10.2 No Re-Litigation at First Stage.** The interview panel is not bound by the first-stage recommendation and conducts its own independent assessment; the first-stage record is provided for continuity, not as a binding input to the interview decision.
**10.3 Feedback Loop.** Where an interview panel's assessment materially diverges from the first-stage decision, that divergence shall be logged to support periodic review of first-stage screening quality under Section 11.

## Section 11. Fairness Monitoring and Audit
**11.1 Periodic Fairness Review.** The AI Governance function shall periodically review a sample of first-stage screening decisions for consistency with this Policy and for any pattern suggesting inconsistent application of the mandatory requirement.
**11.2 Independent Audit.** Internal audit may independently sample screening records under Section 6.1 to confirm that rejections were properly grounded in the mandatory requirement rather than in unsupported or absent evidence.
**11.3 Corrective Action.** Where a review under this Section identifies a systematic issue, either with recruiter practice or with the AI-assisted system's recommendations, the Head of Talent Acquisition and the Head of Model Risk and Governance shall agree corrective action.

## Section 12. Definitions
**12.1 Candidate File.** The complete set of materials submitted by or about a candidate for first-stage screening, including their curriculum vitae and any accompanying certification evidence.
**12.2 Recognised Framework.** A professional certification or assessment framework named in the job description, or formally recognised as equivalent by the AI Governance function.
**12.3 First-Stage Screening.** The initial advance-or-reject decision governed by this Policy, as distinct from the subsequent structured interview referenced in Section 10.
**12.4 Recruiter.** The human decision-maker exercising the final decision authority described in Section 5.

## Section 13. Effective Date and Distribution
**13.1 Effective Date.** This version of the Policy takes effect from its publication date and supersedes any prior version for all first-stage screening conducted after that date.
**13.2 Distribution.** This Policy is distributed to all recruiters and hiring managers involved in first-stage screening of AI governance and Responsible AI roles, and is made available to the AI-assisted screening system as governing material.
**13.3 Queries.** Questions about the interpretation of this Policy, including whether a specific certification satisfies Section 4.1 of the job description, should be directed to the Head of Talent Acquisition rather than resolved informally.
